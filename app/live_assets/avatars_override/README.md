# Avatar overrides

A photo here replaces a leader's X profile photo everywhere in Nazar.

- **File name:** the X handle in lower case, `.jpg`. For example, `rashokabjp.jpg` for @RAshokaBJP.
- **Image:** square, at least 200×200, one face filling most of the frame. No crowds, posters or group photos.
- **Rights:** use a photo you are allowed to use (party press kit, official portrait, or your own), and note its source below.
- **Going live:** commit and push. Render serves it after the next deploy. No restart is needed locally.
